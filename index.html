Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about appointments, services, timing, and visiting the shop. If you need something more specific, we’re only a call or message away.

Ask us a question
Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can reserve time for your vehicle and understand what it needs before you arrive. Call or use our contact form to request a time.

What are your business hours?

Revline Performance Garage is open Monday through Friday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Appointments are recommended.

What automotive services do you provide?

We handle routine maintenance, diagnostics, mechanical repairs, brakes, suspension, electrical work, tires, inspections, performance upgrades, and custom projects.

Will you explain the work before getting started?

Yes. After evaluating your vehicle, we’ll explain what we found, discuss the recommended next steps, and answer your questions before moving forward.

How long will my service take?

Timing depends on the vehicle, the service required, parts availability, and what we find during inspection. We’ll provide a clearer timeframe once we understand the job.

How do I schedule appearance services?

Contact Revline and tell us which service you’re interested in. We’ll help coordinate detailing, coating, paint correction, tint, vinyl, or other appearance work with our trusted partners.

Where is Revline Performance Garage located?

Visit us at 1000 E 73rd Ave, Unit 7313, Denver, CO 80229 . You can also call 720-800-1542 or email admin@revlinepg.com.
