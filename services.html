Prevent problems early

Maintenance & diagnostics

Regular maintenance helps protect reliability, while accurate diagnostics help identify the cause of warning lights, noises, leaks, drivability issues, or changing performance before parts are replaced.

  • Oil and filter changes
  • Factory-scheduled and preventative maintenance
  • Check-engine-light and drivability diagnostics
  • Cooling-system and radiator service
  • Fuel-system cleaning and repair
  • Battery and charging-system testing

Control and stopping confidence

Brake, suspension & steering repair

Squealing or grinding brakes, vibration, uneven tire wear, wandering, clunks, and changes in ride quality can signal that your chassis systems need attention. We inspect the concern and explain the repair options.

  • Brake pad, rotor, and hydraulic service
  • Suspension inspection and repair
  • Steering-component service
  • Shocks, struts, and related components
  • Wheel alignment and balancing

Mechanical repair

Engine, transmission & drivetrain

When a vehicle develops leaks, overheating, shifting concerns, unusual sounds, or a loss of power, the right diagnosis matters. Revline handles mechanical repairs and helps customers understand the scope of work before the repair moves forward.

  • Engine diagnosis and repair
  • Transmission service and repair
  • Timing belt and timing chain replacement
  • Drivetrain and differential service
  • Cooling and lubrication-system repairs
