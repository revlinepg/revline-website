Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how Revline Performance Garage collects, uses, and safeguards information you provide.

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

Depending on how you contact us or use our services, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Information about your vehicle and requested services.
  • Messages submitted through our website contact form.
  • Photos or videos of your vehicle.

How We Use Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate about appointments or services.
  • Prepare service recommendations, invoices, and related records.
  • Improve our services and customer experience.
  • Maintain shop and customer records.

Website Forms and Service Providers

Messages sent through our contact form are processed by Formspree so they can be delivered to Revline. Embedded Instagram and Google Reviews content may also load services operated by SnapWidget, Elfsight, Google, and Instagram. Those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or support requested services, when you authorize it, or when disclosure is required by law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Vehicle Media

Revline may photograph or record vehicles during service for documentation and marketing. If you do not want your vehicle featured publicly, notify us in writing before your appointment.

Your Choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held by Revline, subject to records we must retain for legitimate business or legal purposes.
